# DATA SOURCE MAPPING: Grow More and Stable Life
**Central Market Data Ingestion Architecture & Provider Mapping**

---

## 1. Overview & Core Ingestion Principles

All financial data displayed in **Grow More and Stable Life** originates from verified, authorized external sources or rigorous internal mathematical calculations. In accordance with strict fintech software standards:
1. **No Fabricated Data**: If an external provider is temporarily unavailable or an asset symbol is not found, the system displays an explicit "Delayed / Stale / Data Unavailable" status. Missing values are never silently replaced with fictitious numbers or zeros.
2. **Backend Gateway**: All external API calls are made exclusively via backend services and scheduled worker jobs. No private API keys or sensitive credentials are ever exposed to client-side JavaScript.
3. **Multi-Tier Caching**: Incoming data is validated against schema contracts, cached in Redis with strict TTLs, and persistently snapshot into PostgreSQL.
4. **Auditability & Provenance**: Every price point and indicator carries two timestamps: `source_timestamp` (when generated by the exchange/provider) and `retrieved_at` (when fetched by our system), along with provider attribution.

## 3. Detailed Data Ingestion Specifications

### 3.1 Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) Ingestion
* **Primary Source**: `https://dps.psx.com.pk/` (PSX Data Portal).
* **Endpoints**:
  - Snapshot / Current Market: `https://dps.psx.com.pk/market-watch`
  - Constituents & Symbol Data: `https://dps.psx.com.pk/company/:symbol`
  - Announcements: `https://dps.psx.com.pk/announcements`
* **Backup Source**: Yahoo Finance ticker suffix `.KA` (e.g. `OGDC.KA`, `SYS.KA`, `ENGRO.KA`).
* **Fields Ingested**:
  - `symbol`, `name`, `sector`, `open`, `high`, `low`, `close`, `previous_close`, `change`, `change_pct`, `volume`, `value_traded`, `market_cap`, `pe_ratio`, `dividend_yield`, `ldcp` (Last Day Closing Price).
* **Trading Hours**: Monday–Thursday 09:15–15:30 PKT; Friday 09:15–12:00, 14:30–16:30 PKT.
* **Holidays**: Pakistan national and Islamic holidays tracked in `market_holidays` table.

### 3.2 US Markets Ingestion
* **Primary Source**: Yahoo Finance API (Public Query Engine) & Finnhub.
* **Tracked Indices**: S&P 500 (`^GSPC`), Dow Jones Industrial Average (`^DJI`), NASDAQ Composite (`^IXIC`), NASDAQ 100 (`^NDX`), Russell 2000 (`^RUT`), US Dollar Index (`DX-Y.NYB`), CBOE Volatility Index (`^VIX`).
* **Fields Ingested**: `symbol`, `name`, `current`, `high`, `low`, `change`, `change_pct`, `direction`, `last_updated_time`.
* **Trading Hours**: Monday–Friday 09:30–16:00 EST (New York).

### 3.3 Saudi Stock Exchange (Tadawul) Ingestion
* **Primary Source**: Yahoo Finance ticker suffix `.SR` (e.g. `1010.SR`, `2222.SR`, `TASI.SR`) and Saudi Exchange public disclosures.
* **Tracked Indices**: Tadawul All Share Index (TASI), MSCI Tadawul 30 (MT30), Nomu Parallel Market Capped Index (NOMUC).
* **Trading Hours**: Sunday–Thursday 10:00–15:00 AST (Riyadh). Note: Sunday is an active trading day in Saudi Arabia!

### 3.4 Pakistan Mutual Funds (MUFAP) Ingestion
* **Primary Source**: Mutual Funds Association of Pakistan (`https://www.mufap.com.pk/`).
* **Frequency**: Nightly ingestion at 21:00 PKT after asset management companies declare end-of-day NAVs.
* **Fields Ingested**:
  - `fund_symbol`, `fund_name`, `amc_name`, `category` (Equity, Money Market, Income, Shariah Compliant, Balanced, Asset Allocation), `nav`, `nav_date`, `inception_date`, `aum_pkr`, `ytd_return`, `1m_return`, `3m_return`, `6m_return`, `1y_return`, `dividend_yield`.

### 3.5 Precious Metals & Gold/Silver Bullion Ingestion
* **Dual-Pricing Framework**:
  1. **Spot-Derived Rates**: Calculated dynamically in real time from international spot bullion (`GC=F` USD/oz) and interbank `USD/PKR` exchange rate:
     $$\text{Gold PKR per Gram} = \frac{\text{Spot Gold USD} \times \text{USD/PKR}}{31.1035} \times \text{Karat Purity Ratio}$$
     $$\text{Gold PKR per Tola} = \text{Gold PKR per Gram} \times 11.6638$$
  2. **Official Sarafa Rates**: Local market closing prices published daily by the All Pakistan Sarafa Gems and Jewellers Association (APGJSA) for 24 Karat and 22 Karat (per tola and per 10 grams), with canonical source article URLs recorded.

### 3.6 Macroeconomic Indicators Ingestion
* **Official Sources**: State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) and Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS).
* **Tracked Metrics**:
  - SBP Policy Rate (Target Rate) & Historical Decisions.
  - Headline Inflation (CPI YoY and MoM) & Core Inflation (NFNE).
  - Foreign Exchange Reserves (SBP Reserves + Commercial Bank Reserves).
  - Current Account Balance (Monthly Deficit / Surplus in USD Millions).
  - Trade Balance (Exports vs Imports).
  - USD/PKR Interbank Closing Rate.
  - Large Scale Manufacturing (LSM) Index.
  - Real GDP Growth Rate (Annual).

## 4. Ingestion Resilience & Fallback Plan

1. **Circuit Breakers & Retries**:
   - Ingestion jobs use exponential backoff (1s, 2s, 4s, 8s) with a maximum of 3 retries.
   - If an external provider returns consecutive HTTP 429 (Too Many Requests) or 5xx server errors, the circuit breaker opens for 5 minutes, routing requests to the secondary provider.
2. **Graceful Stale State Handling**:
   - When a fresh live quote cannot be retrieved, the system serves the cached quote from Redis with `is_stale: true` and the exact `last_updated_at` timestamp.
   - An alert is logged in `api_logs` for administrator review.
3. **Data Integrity Checks**:
   - Incoming prices must satisfy sanity checks: $\text{Price} > 0$, $\left| \Delta \% \right| < 100\%$ within a single tick (unless confirmed as a stock split or circuit-breaker halt).
   - Any anomalous tick is quarantined in `data_review_queue` and does not corrupt historical databases.
